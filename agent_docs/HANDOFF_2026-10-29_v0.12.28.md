## v0.12.28 — calmer Command Center

- **Dashboard views.** The Command Center splits into *This week*, *Players & goals* and *League*. The weekly view is about 40% shorter; agenda rows that point at a decision panel collapse to one line.
- **Game Lab.** One matchup header instead of three; "Game Day Intelligence" is now a matchup breakdown.
- **Recruiting.** The board comes first; workspace cards gain portraits and staff-confidence bars, and open on All Recruits when My Board is empty.
- **Game settings (Program Lab).** Per-dynasty administration patience, injury rate and recruiting edge. Defaults are neutral, so existing saves play exactly as before.
- **Career milestones (Program Lab).** Thirteen milestones read from your tenure record, remembered by season.
- **Fixes.** Surfaces such as the Game Day hero no longer sit mid-animation (dim team names); Game Day and shell labels raised from 7-9px to 10-11px; "Opening week" replaces "Week 0"; quieter ticker; "Year one" History for new dynasties.

No save-shape changes beyond two additive fields (`settings`, `milestones`); simulation and balance are unchanged at the default settings.
