### Presentation and features

- Season card: last result and streak include archived postseason games; phase outlook replaces "No remaining regular-season game" (`season-presentation-adapter.js`, `gameday-season-presentation.js`). Test: `tests/season-presentation-postseason.js`.
- Dashboard week strip: CCG/BOWL/R16/QF/SF/TITLE chips from this season's archive (`brand-snapshot-adapter.js`, `brand-visuals.js`); strip now actually scrolls to the next game (it measured its non-overflowing host before), or the latest result after Week 12.
- Postseason finals posted to `#saveStatus` (ticker/toast/sting) from both the legacy and Game Engine 2 postseason paths (`announceUserPostseason` in `app.js`, called from `game-engine-v2-postseason.js`). Test: `tests/postseason-status.js`.
- `#weekLine` names the stage after the regular season (Conference championships, Bowl season, Playoff, Season complete, Offseason). HUD rank arrow resets on a new season (`game-hud.js`).
- History: tracked header includes a finished season until the offseason review writes `allTimeRecord`; new Dynasty Arc chart (`world-history-presentation*.{js,css}`). Tests in `tests/dynasty-history.js`.
- Transfer portal renders 40 rows at a time with a Show more row; targets and your commitments always shown; filters reset paging.
