# v0.11 — Program Builder and Athletic Department Economy

## Goal

Convert institutional ratings from editable numbers into long-term allocation
decisions and move Create-a-School into new-dynasty setup.

## New-dynasty builder

Support name, nickname, city/state, colors, logo/mark choice, protected rival,
conference placement, schemes and institutional archetype. Use a fixed creation
budget so strengths require weaknesses. Replace one fictional program initially;
do not change league size in the first slice.

## Annual economy

Create a budget from resources, prestige, administration support and recent
performance. Allocate among coaching payroll, recruiting operations, NIL
infrastructure, facilities, development, sports medicine and academics.

Each investment needs:

- immediate cost;
- multi-year level or project duration;
- maintenance cost where appropriate;
- a visible, bounded effect on existing calculations;
- administration consequences for chronic deficits.

Do not add a second ratings system. Investments should modify or gradually move
the existing institutional fields.

## Facilities projects

Offer a small generated list with cost, duration, effect and risk. Construction
should complete across seasons, survive coaching changes, and appear in program
history. Administration confidence may unlock funding but should not create free
upgrades every year.

## Tests and calibration

- Budget conservation and no negative unreported balances.
- AI allocation strategies by institutional archetype.
- Small programs can improve slowly but cannot reach universal 100s quickly.
- Maintenance prevents permanent runaway advantage.
- Twelve- and twenty-season prestige/resource distributions remain healthy.

## Delivery slices

1. v0.11.0: pre-dynasty school builder and Commissioner separation.
2. v0.11.1: annual budget and AI allocations.
3. v0.11.2: facilities projects and administration funding.
4. v0.11.3: history presentation and long-run calibration.

## Exit criteria

Program growth comes from constrained, explainable investments; custom schools
enter the existing 120-team universe safely; institutional strengths produce
different strategies without creating an obvious universal allocation.

## Non-goals

No ticket-price spreadsheet, donor micromanagement, stadium 3D editor or league-
size changes.
