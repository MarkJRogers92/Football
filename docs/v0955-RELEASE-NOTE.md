# Dynasty Lab v0.9.55 — Staff, Recruiting Battles and Development Plans

v0.9.55 deepens three linked decision loops without exposing hidden truth.

## Staff scouting identity
Coaches now carry deterministic evaluation specialties tied to durable coach identity. Relevant expertise improves evaluation efficiency, while the Staff Evaluation Ledger grades only settled observed outcomes from prior signees.

## Recruiting battles
Recruiting races now show race position, leader gap, momentum, decision pressure, challenged commitments and short battle history. The controlled program receives three Priority Pushes per week; each adds a bounded +4 pitch boost for that week.

## Featured development plans
Up to five players per offseason can receive Featured Development Plans using the existing training-focus system. Plans concentrate development attempts rather than increasing hidden upside or guaranteeing gains, and spring/fall receipts preserve what actually happened.

Hidden true ratings, hidden upside, growth profile/volatility and other anti-cheat information remain excluded from player-facing scouting judgments and historical staff grades.
